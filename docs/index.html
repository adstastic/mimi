Hands-free dictation for macOS. Leave the mic armed, say what you want, and the words land wherever your cursor is.

Switch it on and talk. mimi does the rest.

Fully local

Speech becomes text on your Mac. No account, no upload, no telemetry. Recordings are discarded once they're transcribed.

Ambient modevoiceprint

Nothing to hold. mimi starts when you start talking and stops when you pause. Enroll your voice once and it keeps only your speech — a call on speaker, a video, someone else in the room, none of it reaches your text. Prefer a key? Hold Right Command.

Agent sessions, hands off

Turn on send with Return and ambient mode becomes a loop: you speak, mimi inserts the text and hits Return, your agent runs. Answer the next prompt from across the room.

Clone the repo and run scripts/run_app.sh. Grant mic and accessibility, then start talking. Requires macOS with Apple on-device speech

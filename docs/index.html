mimidictation for macOS

Leave the mic armed. Just talk.

mimi is dictation for macOS that doesn't need a key held down. In ambient mode it listens for speech, starts on its own, stops when you pause, and puts the text where your cursor is. All on your Mac — no account, no upload, no telemetry.

Any text fieldReady

Turn on ambient mode and wait, or hold the key and release.

Ambient mode

No key to hold

The mic stays armed. mimi watches for speech, starts recording when you begin, and ends the take when you pause. Your hands never leave what they were doing.

Speech detection, not a timer

Apple's speech detector decides where a take starts and ends, so a thinking pause mid-sentence doesn't cut you off and a silent room doesn't fill with empty transcripts.

Still keyed when you want it

Hold Right Command for one deliberate take, or tap once to run hands-free without arming the mic all day. Escape cancels either.

Voiceprint filtering experimental

Enroll your voice once

Record a couple of samples in Settings → My Voice. mimi builds a profile of how you sound and keeps it on the machine.

Other voices get dropped

With a profile saved, mimi splits a recording by speaker, keeps the segments that match you, and transcribes only those. A call on speaker or a colleague nearby stops landing in your text.

What to expect

This one is honest about its state: it works, not always. Overlapping speech and short takes are where it slips. Verify your profile after enrolling, and reset it if matches drift.

The rest of it

Recognition on-device

Apple's on-device speech engine handles the stream, with a local Parakeet model as fallback. English only, no round trip to a server.

Insert where you're typing

Text goes into the focused field, optionally followed by Return. Every transcript is copied to the clipboard too, so a bad paste target costs you nothing.

Tune it to your setup

Pick the microphone, choose how takes end, set the silence threshold. A live overlay shows partial text while you speak.

Ways to start a take

ambient
Mic stays armed. mimi starts on speech and ends on a pause. Nothing to press.
hold ⌘
Speak while you hold Right Command. Release and the text is inserted.
tap ⌘
Tap once, speak hands-free. mimi stops on a pause, or on a second tap.
esc
Cancel a recording. Nothing is transcribed, nothing is inserted.

What leaves your Mac

Audio: nothing

Recordings are transcribed locally and discarded. There is no mimi server to send them to.

Usage: nothing

No analytics, no crash pings, no account.

One-time downloads

Apple's speech assets and the fallback model download once. After that, dictation works offline.

Run it

git clone https://github.com/adimuk/mimi
cd mimi
scripts/run_app.sh

Grant microphone and accessibility permission when asked, focus a text field, and hold Right Command.

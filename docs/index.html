mimihands-free dictation

Talk to your Mac. It types.

Hands-free dictation for macOS. Leave the mic armed, say what you want, and the words land wherever your cursor is.

Any text fieldReady

Switch it on and talk. mimi does the rest.

◍

Fully local

Speech becomes text on your Mac. No account, no upload, no telemetry.

◉

Ambient mode

Nothing to hold. mimi starts when you start talking and stops when you pause.

⌇

Voiceprintbeta

Enroll your voice once and mimi keeps only your speech. Someone else in the room never reaches your text.

⏎

Agent sessions, hands off

Send with Return makes it a loop: you speak, mimi types and hits Return, your agent runs.

⌘

Or use the key

Hold Right Command for one deliberate take, or tap it to run hands-free. Escape cancels.

▤

Lands where you are

Text goes into the focused field and onto the clipboard, so a bad paste target costs nothing.

Clone the repo and run scripts/run_app.sh. Grant mic and accessibility, then start talking. Requires macOS with Apple on-device speech
